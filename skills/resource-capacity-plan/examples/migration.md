# Resource Capacity Plan: migration example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Worked comparison: a rehearsal week

This is an instructional capacity subcase. Chen has forty gross hours, eight hours of leave, and four overhead hours, leaving 28. Migration rehearsal requires 24 hours and existing-system support requires eight, creating a four-hour overload. Beck's vendor capacity is unconfirmed and must not be entered as free replacement capacity.

## Recommendation

Jules asks Chen's manager whether four support hours can move to a qualified colleague during the rehearsal. Until confirmed, report the rehearsal plan as resource-constrained. If the work requires Chen on two parallel critical activities, examine the daily sequence as well as this weekly total.

## Why this works

The decision is a specific coverage change with a named authority. It does not assume overtime, vendor availability, or interchangeable skills.

## Repair

**Flawed:** "Use the vendor for the remaining four hours."

**Corrected:** "Vendor availability and competency for these tasks are unknown. Obtain confirmation or adjust demand; keep the unconfirmed option separate from the committed plan."
