# Northstar: a date change does not pass a gate

Fictional artifact as of 25 November 2026. Noel approved M-CR02 on 9 November: phased migration, 28 November cutover and 300k USD B2 performance budget. Restore demonstration has now failed. Saira's business and Rosa's service acceptance remain distinct.

| Gate or decision | Evidence needed | Actual state on 25 November | Authority / next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revised scope/date/funding | M-CR02 terms and authorized funding | Approved as recorded | Noel's decision preserved; not overwritten by a failed rehearsal |
| Business acceptance | Correct agreed population, explained mismatches, applicable workflow evidence | Final successful rehearsal/acceptance not yet recorded | Saira evaluates new evidence after remediation |
| Recovery readiness | Successful restore demonstration applicable to planned cutover | Failed | Chen coordinates technical repair; the failure remains in history |
| Cutover go/no-go | Applicable readiness and acceptance evidence | Hold recommendation | Actual go decision must be recorded; no premature use of 28 November's later decision |
| Service responsibility transfer | Restore, monitoring, support ownership and accepted residuals | Rosa has not accepted | Keep delivery-to-operations boundary explicit |

## Forum design

Use a bounded readiness review with Chen's execution evidence, Saira's acceptance position and Rosa's service concerns. Jules prepares the options and unresolved items. A proposed meeting time or participant list is not evidence that the review occurred. Record go, hold or conditional action with exact conditions and authority; do not take a majority vote to erase required restore evidence.

If remediation cannot support the revised date, prepare a new forecast and decision brief. M-CR02 remains the approved comparison until changed. Financial authorization does not grant permission to skip independent acceptance. No general numerical tolerance or emergency deputy is supplied, so both remain unresolved in the governance agreement.

**Repair:** “All gates green because M-CR02 approved” confuses authorization to pursue a plan with evidence that its release conditions were met. Keep each decision and gate on its own row, with current evidence and a visible hold branch.
