# Northstar: lead a supplier participant through project governance

Fictional training scenario, 6 November 2026. After lost attachment links were observed, supplier analyst Chen delivered a revised mapping but did not provide the promised affected-population and exception report before the reconciliation review. Jules coordinates the migration. Beck is the supplier lead, Saira owns business acceptance, Rosa owns service acceptance and Noel holds material plan/funding authority.

Chen is a supplier participant, not Jules's direct report. Contract terms and Chen's employer processes are not supplied.

## Evidence and diagnosis

| Signal | Evidence | Working diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| Revised mapping delivered | Supplier output exists | Some technical progress |
| Population and exception report late | Promised input absent at review | Observable missed project commitment |
| Acceptance blocked | Saira lacks reconciliation evidence | Material project consequence |
| Cause | Capacity, clarity, data access and supplier priority unknown | Diagnose before attributing intent |
| Authority | Beck represents supplier management; Jules coordinates project | Use supplier governance for allocation or formal action |

Recommended sequence: clarify the deliverable and acceptance evidence with Beck and Chen, remove data/access obstacles, give factual project feedback, create a short practice/review loop, then use supplier governance if the accepted commitment fails again.

## Delegation and interface brief

**Purpose:** provide usable evidence for business reconciliation and downstream restore planning.

**Supplier outcome:** affected-population and exception report tied to the revised mapping version, with method, coverage, unresolved exceptions and source date.

**Boundary:** the report does not establish Saira's acceptance or Rosa's service readiness. Commercial completion remains governed by the contract and authorized supplier process.

**Decision rights:** Chen prepares technical evidence within the supplier's accepted assignment. Beck confirms supplier priority and resources. Saira determines whether the business evidence is acceptable. Jules coordinates the interface and reports consequences.

**Inputs:** mapping version, source population, exception definition, data access and review format. Their availability must be confirmed.

**Checkpoint:** a sample report is reviewed before the full reconciliation package. Exact dates and supplier capacity remain proposed until Beck and Chen accept them.

## Feedback conversation

Jules to Chen and Beck:

“The revised mapping was delivered, but the affected-population and exception report promised for the reconciliation review was not available. Without it, Saira cannot determine what was corrected or which records remain unresolved. I want to check whether the expected evidence, data access, priority or timing was unclear. What prevented the report from being ready, and what can the supplier responsibly commit to now?”

Questions:

- Which deliverable fields or coverage rules need clarification?
- Is the source population available and stable?
- What supplier capacity and review support has Beck accepted?
- What sample can demonstrate the method before the full report?
- When should Jules surface a risk rather than wait for the due date?

The conversation seeks facts and a recoverable commitment. Jules does not threaten Chen's employment or issue a supplier performance rating.

## Support actions

| Need | Action | Owner / authority | Success evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence definition | Saira states the business reconciliation questions and usable report conditions | Saira | Written criterion and sample disposition |
| Data access | Confirm source population and access route | Technical/data owner to confirm | Reproducible population |
| Supplier priority | Confirm capacity and review time | Beck | Accepted supplier commitment |
| Interface coordination | Track version, questions and decisions in one record | Jules | Current evidence register |

## Project-development plan

Project skill: producing receiver-usable evidence, not merely a technical output.

Practice: Chen prepares a small sample tied to one mapping version and explains population, method, exceptions and uncertainty. Saira reviews usability; Jules observes the handoff and gives factual feedback. If the sample is accepted, Chen applies the pattern to the full package.

This is a project work-development loop. Beck decides any formal supplier coaching or people action.

## Review checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Sample definition | Version, population, method and exceptions visible | Correct misunderstanding before scale |
| Sample review | Saira records usable or unusable with reason | Proceed or revise |
| Full package | Coverage and exceptions reconcile to the agreed population | Route business acceptance |
| Repeated miss after accepted inputs/support | Factual chronology and migration impact | Use supplier governance and escalate the contract/delivery decision |

**Repair:** “Chen missed the report, so Jules should put Chen on a performance plan” ignores the cross-organizational boundary. The corrected response leads the project interface and routes formal supplier management through Beck and the contract.
