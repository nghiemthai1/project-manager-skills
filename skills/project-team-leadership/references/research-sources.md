## Method foundations

- Outcome-based delegation with explicit decision rights, constraints, resources and review.
- Feedback grounded in observable behavior, project impact and a concrete next action.
- Coaching through a specific skill, practice opportunity, observation and feedback loop.
- Project governance that keeps delivery coordination separate from formal employment authority.
- Psychological safety and accountability as complementary operating conditions.

Framework names and source review do not establish endorsement, certification or field reliability.
