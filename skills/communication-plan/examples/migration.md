# Northstar communications at a failed restore gate

Fictional training scenario, as of 25 November 2026. M-CR02 on 9 November authorized phased scope and B2: 28 November cutover and 300k performance budget. The restore demonstration failed today. A routine vendor completion notice cannot establish release readiness.

| Audience / decision | Evidence | Proposed sender and timing | Closure and fallback |
|---|---|---|---|

| Service desk agents / operating instructions | Actual authorized window, user actions and support route | Authorized communicator to confirm; accessible shift-specific channel | Operator understanding/action; do not issue a success notice from a forecast |

Draft decision message: “Restore failed on 25 November. Hold readiness pending correction and applicable evidence. B2 remains the authorized comparison; no new cutover date has been approved in this record. Confirm the evidence and decision review needed before the 28 November window.” Exact retest capacity and last responsible decision time remain gaps.

The plan separates restricted technical results from the audience action notice. Both use the same decision state; operators do not receive “confirmed cutover” while the authority sees “hold.” If shift availability is unknown, record the coverage/communication gap instead of assuming one daytime message reaches everyone.

Later history stays later: successful rehearsal and Saira's acceptance on 27 November, Noel's go/cutover on 28 November and Rosa's handover on 2 December are subsequent records. None is inferred from sending the 25 November brief.

Review whether the failed-gate message reached the actual decider in useful time and whether operators understood the final instructions. Preserve disagreements and corrections so an old vendor notice cannot remain the apparent source of truth.

**Repair:** “Vendor says complete; reuse the message for users and operations” loses both evidence and purpose. The separate rows above retain common facts with the required response for each audience.
