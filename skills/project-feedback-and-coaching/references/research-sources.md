This package is an original software and IT project-management synthesis. It was informed by a quality review of existing leadership skills and uses original wording, structure, templates and examples.

## Method foundations

- Feedback grounded in observable behavior, project impact and a concrete next action.
- Coaching through a specific skill, practice opportunity, observation and review loop.
- Project governance that keeps delivery feedback separate from formal employment authority.
- Psychological safety and accountability as complementary project conditions.
- Privacy boundaries that prevent project records becoming informal personnel files.

Framework names and source review do not establish endorsement, certification or field reliability.
