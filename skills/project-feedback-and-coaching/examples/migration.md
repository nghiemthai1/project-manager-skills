# Northstar: coach receiver-usable supplier evidence

Fictional training scenario, 6 November 2026. Supplier analyst Chen delivered a revised mapping after lost attachment links were observed, but the affected-population and exception report promised for reconciliation review was absent. Jules coordinates the migration. Beck is the supplier lead, Saira owns business acceptance, Rosa owns service acceptance and Noel holds material plan and funding authority.

Chen is a supplier participant, not Jules's direct report. Contract terms, supplier capacity and Chen's employer processes are not supplied.

## Evidence and route

| Signal | Evidence | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Revised mapping | Supplier output exists | Technical progress occurred |
| Missing report | Promised input absent at review | Observable delivery behavior and project consequence |
| Acceptance effect | Saira lacks population and exception evidence | Business reconciliation cannot be decided |
| Cause | Capacity, clarity, access and priority unknown | Ask before attributing intent |
| Authority | Beck represents supplier management; Jules coordinates the interface | Formal supplier action belongs through Beck and governance |

Recommended sequence: give factual interface feedback with Beck present, clarify the receiver-usable evidence, provide a small practice example, review it with Saira and route any repeated supplier commitment failure through governance.

## Feedback conversation

Jules to Chen and Beck:

“The revised mapping was delivered, but the affected-population and exception report promised for reconciliation review was not available. Without it, Saira cannot determine what was corrected or which records remain unresolved. I want to check whether the evidence definition, data access, priority or timing was unclear. What prevented the report from being ready, and what can the supplier responsibly commit to now?”

Questions:

- Which fields or coverage rules need clarification?
- Is the source population available and stable?
- What supplier capacity and review support has Beck accepted?
- What sample could demonstrate the method before the full report?
- When should a delivery risk be surfaced rather than held for the due date?

The conversation seeks facts and an observable next behavior. Jules does not threaten Chen's employment or issue a supplier performance rating.

## Coaching plan

**Project skill:** producing receiver-usable evidence rather than only a technical output.

**Competent behavior:** tie one mapping version to its source population, method, coverage, exceptions and uncertainty so the receiver can make a bounded acceptance decision.

**Practice:** Chen prepares a small sample for one mapping version and explains population, method and unresolved exceptions. Saira reviews usability. Jules observes the handoff and gives factual feedback. If the sample is usable, Chen applies the pattern to the full package.

**Support:** Saira supplies business reconciliation questions; the data owner confirms population and access; Beck confirms supplier priority and review time.

**Review evidence:** receiver disposition, traceable population, visible exceptions and timely disclosure of blockers.

## Checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Sample definition | Version, population, method and exceptions visible | Correct misunderstanding before scale |
| Sample review | Saira records usable or unusable with reason | Recognize learning or revise practice |
| Full package | Same method produces receiver-usable coverage | Close coaching and route acceptance |
| Repeated miss after clear evidence and accepted support | Factual chronology and migration impact | Use supplier governance for the delivery or contract decision |

**Repair:** putting Chen on a project-created performance plan would cross the organizational boundary. The corrected loop develops project work while Beck retains supplier-management authority.
