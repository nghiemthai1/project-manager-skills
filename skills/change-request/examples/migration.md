Compare maintaining the current scope/sequence, phasing with a revised baseline, and a further timing/resource response if the phased plan is infeasible. Exact option estimates and detailed deliberation are not supplied. The recommendation is the evidenced phased change, with its implementation details and acceptance consequences made explicit; it is not a claim that any arbitrary phase split will work.
