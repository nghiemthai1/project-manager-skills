# Project Closure: software example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Relay closure: 10 November

The report compares B1's 30 October pilot and USD 100,000 performance budget with CR-001's scope/B2 funding decision and D-004's date deferral. The pilot began 3 November; Theo accepted service handover on 6 November. Ada's closure approval is recorded on 10 November.

The scenario does not supply final actual costs. The report therefore labels financial-close evidence as not supplied rather than using B2 USD 120,000 as actual spend. A real closure packet would need the financial reconciliation or an explicitly accepted transfer of the remaining obligation.

Priya owns the 4 December benefit review. That future review does not mean benefits were realized at closure.
