# Project Closure: migration example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Northstar closure: 4 December

The report preserves B1's 21 November cutover and USD 240,000 baseline, then explains M-CR02's phased plan, 28 November date, and B2 USD 300,000. Cutover occurred 28 November, Rosa accepted handover on 2 December, and closure was approved on 4 December.

Saira owns the 15 January 2027 benefit review. Archive access and any residual vendor obligations need named accepted owners. The scenario does not provide invoice reconciliation or a complete residual-item register; a worked report must state that limitation rather than fabricate clean closure evidence.

The existence of a recorded closure approval is a fact of the scenario. It does not license the analyst to invent the documents underlying that decision.

## Repair

**Flawed:** "No open invoices or issues remain; all benefits achieved."

**Corrected:** "Report the supplied approval and handover evidence, identify missing financial/residual records, and preserve the separately owned future benefit review."
