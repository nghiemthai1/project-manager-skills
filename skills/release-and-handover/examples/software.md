# Release and Handover: software example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Relay transition

D-004 defers the pilot to 3 November. Security evidence is accepted on 2 November. The pilot starts on 3 November with a defined tenant scope and explicit operational coverage. The execution record captures the actual rollout result and relevant monitoring; it does not mark completion merely because the go decision exists.

Theo accepts full service handover on 6 November after the agreed operating evidence and materials are ready. Mina retains the transition record and outstanding obligations for 10 November closure. Priya owns the later benefit review.

The workflow does not invent rollback thresholds or claim a demonstration from the presence of a runbook. Those details must come from the actual release plan and evidence.

## Repair

**Flawed:** "Deployment succeeded, so archive the project and release everyone."

**Corrected:** "Verify service behavior, maintain interim coverage, obtain handover acceptance, and only then bring the project to its separate closure decision."
