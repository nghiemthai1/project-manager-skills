Before execution, the plan needs named monitoring, incident and recovery decision owners, access and availability. A prepared runbook is not a demonstration, and a rollback promise must account for actual data/external effects. Where the source lacks those details, this packet identifies retrieval gaps rather than filling them with plausible names or thresholds.

Handover inventory: current runbook and recovery limits; operator access; monitoring/escalation routes; applicable training/guidance; known issues and accepted residual obligations. The inventory is proposed structure; actual delivered versions and accepted item-by-item duties require the source record. Sending it to Theo would not by itself establish his acceptance.

At 6 November, the known service acceptance can be recorded as fact while the analyst still notes missing supporting attachments. Do not erase that supplied decision because the packet is incomplete, and do not invent clean evidence underneath it.
