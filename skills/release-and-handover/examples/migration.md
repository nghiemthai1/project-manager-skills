# Northstar cutover and accepted operations transfer

Fictional training scenario. M-CR02 authorizes the phased plan and 28 November baseline. A failed restore on 25 November leads to hold/remediation; successful rehearsal and Saira's acceptance on 27 November support Noel's actual go for 28 November. Rosa accepts full operations handover on 2 December.

| Transition | Evidence/decision recorded | What still needs the underlying execution record |
|---|---|---|
| Readiness after failure | Applicable successful rehearsal and business acceptance on 27 November | Exact configuration, population, limitations and domain-specific criteria |
| Go | Noel's decision for the defined 28 November cutover | Conditions, actual decision reference and user authorization for any live tool action |
| Execution | Cutover occurs 28 November | Actual executed population, step results, reconciliation and deviations |
| Interim operation | Explicit coverage is required until full transfer | Named roster, access, monitoring, incident/recovery routes and observations |
| Handover | Rosa accepts operations on 2 December | Scoped duty/material/residual acceptance evidence |

Proposed execution checkpoints include usable input validation, confirmation of backup/restore prerequisites, inspection before irreversible data changes, post-load relationship reconciliation and service-function verification. Their exact order, performer and recovery decision times depend on the actual runbook; they are not supplied facts or universal steps to execute blindly.

Recovery review distinguishes restoring data from undoing external effects. The plan must show what happens if a later checkpoint fails after some users or downstream systems have acted. Do not assume the earlier restore demonstration covers untested populations or all post-cutover consequences.

Transfer materials include versioned restore procedures, access/monitoring/support routes, operator training, known issues and archive access. Each responsibility needs Rosa or another actual receiving authority's bounded acceptance. Vendor completion does not automatically move ownership to operations, and a report prepared before cutover cannot contain actual stabilization results.

The 4 December project closure is a separate supplied decision. Saira retains the 15 January 2027 benefit review; residual vendor/record obligations require accepted owners rather than disappearing when the project team disbands.

**Repair:** “Vendor cutover complete, operations owns everything” confuses delivery with transfer. The corrected packet records actual execution, continued coverage and the later receiving-owner decision without inventing its missing attachments.
