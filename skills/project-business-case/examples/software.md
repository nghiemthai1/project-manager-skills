# Relay: conditional pilot business case

Fictional instructional appraisal dated 29 September 2026, before D-001. The following option costs and benefit estimates are additional teaching assumptions, not approved Relay budgets or measured results. Ada is the proposed investment decision maker; Mina prepares the comparison. The decision is whether to investigate and authorize a bounded SAML pilot, not whether to promise an enterprise rollout.

## Common comparison basis

One year from assumed go-live; USD thousands; undiscounted, with initial costs at launch and one year's recurring costs included. Benefits below are claimed capacity value, not cash released. No verified support baseline, adoption evidence or discount rate has been supplied, so this is not an NPV calculation or a funding approval.

| Option | Initial cost | Annual recurring cost | Annual gross capacity value assumption | First-year net capacity value less cost | Principal limit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Maintain current approach | 0 incremental | 0 incremental | 0 incremental | 0 | Existing service cost and risk continue; their absolute values are unknown |
| One-tenant SAML pilot | 100 | 12 | 120 | 8 | Claimed support capacity saving unverified |
| Broader rollout | 180 | 24 | 210 | 6 | More users and interfaces; feasibility and benefit assumptions weaker |

The pilot's 8 = 120 − 100 − 12; the broader rollout's 6 = 210 − 180 − 24. The figures do not mean either option generates cash profit. If pilot gross capacity value is 20% lower, its first-year result becomes 96 − 112 = −16. To break even on this narrow comparison it needs 112, or 93.3% of the assumed 120. A delayed launch could reduce the first-year benefit period and reverse the result even without a build overrun.

## Recommendation and evidence request

Recommend validating support demand, achievable time reduction and actual redeployment of released capacity before treating the modeled return as decisive. A bounded pilot may still be justified by learning and customer access needs, but those reasons should be explicit. Keep SCIM outside the proposed scope.
