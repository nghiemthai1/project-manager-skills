# Northstar: reassess continuation when cost changes

Fictional artifact as of 23 October 2026. B1 under M-D001 is 240k USD performance budget plus 24k separate management reserve, with 21 November cutover. Cumulative PV 80k, EV 60k, AC 75k. CPI = 60/75 = 0.8; continuing that efficiency gives EAC = 240/0.8 = 300k. This is a forecast scenario, not a revised authorized budget.

## Decision comparison

| Option | Future financial basis | Outcome and risk | Evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continue B1 scope under current efficiency | Forecast total 300k; estimated remaining spend 225k after 75k actual | Preserves intended boundary, but affordability and finish feasibility unresolved | Updated remaining-work estimates, network and benefit case |
| Phase the migration | Unknown until population, interfaces and transition overlap are defined | Could reduce near-term exposure, but duplicate operation and later work may cost more | Accepted phase boundaries, full-life cost, business/service implications |
| Pause or terminate | 75k spent remains history; future obligations, cancellation and retention cost unknown | Avoids some future delivery spend but may prolong current-service costs and lose intended outcomes | Actual supplier obligations, minimum safe service option and sponsor decision |

The 300k scenario exceeds the 264k total current envelope by 36k; access to the 24k reserve itself still follows its control rules. The business case cannot claim the project is affordable simply because the reserve exists. A cost-based EAC supplies no new cutover date and no proof that benefits still exceed costs.

Report the 75k actual spend transparently but decide using future consequences rather than arguing that prior expenditure makes stopping impossible.
