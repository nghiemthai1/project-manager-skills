## Trial and trigger

The next rehearsal should yield an inspectable population/relationship report and recovery findings, not merely a completion percentage. Track what was actually tested, blocked and learned. If vendor mappings change repeatedly, tighten the interface agreement and reassess the near-term plan. If required work cannot fit, seek an evidenced baseline decision; “hybrid” is not a waiver.
