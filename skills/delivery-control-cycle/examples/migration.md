# Delivery Control Cycle: migration example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Northstar sequence

The 23 October review identifies a funding forecast gap and vendor timing uncertainty. On 6 November the rehearsal supplies new evidence: actual broken links. The cycle creates M-I002, links it to M-R01, updates the forecast basis, and prepares M-CR02.

Noel's 9 November decision changes the plan and funding. The 25 November restore failure is a fresh readiness exception under that plan, not a reason to rewrite the approval. The 27 November successful evidence supports a subsequent go decision; operational handover and closure remain separate later events.

## Repair

**Flawed:** "The plan was approved, so carry last week's green status through cutover."

**Corrected:** "Inspect current gate evidence each cycle and on material changes, regardless of earlier planning approvals."
