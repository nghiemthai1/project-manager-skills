# Delivery Control Cycle: software example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## 16 October cycle

Mina reviews B1, costs through 16 October, the platform forecast, the environment issue, and acceptance evidence. The interface is expected 22 October against a 20 October need. The budget indicators support a conditional EAC of USD 120,000 against B1 USD 100,000.

The cycle keeps I-001 separate from R-001, requests schedule recovery evidence, and prepares a scope/funding decision. The executive update describes these conditions without silently adopting a new budget or date.

At the next review, CR-001's 19 October approval updates scope and budget prospectively. The earlier report remains intact. The later 28 October failed readiness condition triggers an exception review rather than waiting for the normal cadence.

## Repair

**Flawed:** "Update every document to the newest date and call the project aligned."

**Corrected:** "Reconcile current facts while preserving baseline history, approval scope, and period-specific reports."
