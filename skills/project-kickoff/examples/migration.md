Included work covers active tickets, agreed attachments, access mapping, reconciliation, support training and archive access. Historical analytics redesign stays excluded. Noel holds material baseline/funding authority; Saira accepts business results; Rosa accepts operational handover. Jules coordinates the project, Chen leads migration work and Beck is the vendor contact. Contact status alone does not prove contractual commitment authority.

The future M-R01 concern about attachment identifiers is a risk hypothesis here; the 6 November observed failure M-I002 must not be imported into this kickoff as known evidence.
