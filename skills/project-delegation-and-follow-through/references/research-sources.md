## Method foundations

- Outcome-based delegation with explicit decisions, constraints, resources and review.
- Confirmation that separates a proposed assignment from an accepted commitment.
- Checkpoints whose frequency changes with project risk and demonstrated readiness.
- Project governance that keeps delivery coordination separate from employment and supplier-management authority.

Framework names and source review do not establish endorsement, certification or field reliability.
