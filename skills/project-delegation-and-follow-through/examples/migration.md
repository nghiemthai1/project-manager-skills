# Northstar: delegate supplier evidence through the interface

Fictional training scenario, 6 November 2026. After lost attachment links were observed, supplier analyst Chen delivered a revised mapping but not the promised affected-population and exception report before reconciliation review. Jules coordinates the migration. Beck is the supplier lead, Saira owns business acceptance, Rosa owns service acceptance and Noel holds material plan and funding authority.

Chen is a supplier participant, not Jules's direct report. Contract terms, accepted supplier capacity and Chen's employer processes are not supplied.

## Commitment diagnosis

| Condition | Evidence | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Required result | Population and exception evidence needed for reconciliation | Outcome is known at a high level |
| Acceptance | A report was promised for the review | Date appears accepted; content and feasibility need read-back |
| Inputs | Population, exception rules and access are not confirmed | Material feasibility gap |
| Supplier capacity | Not supplied | Beck must confirm priority and resources |
| Acceptance authority | Saira owns business acceptance | Jules coordinates but cannot accept the evidence |
| Formal people authority | Chen works for the supplier | Supplier governance applies |

Recommended sequence: clarify the receiver-usable evidence, obtain supplier and input confirmations, review a sample before scale, then use supplier governance if the accepted commitment misses again.

## Delegation and interface brief

**Purpose:** provide usable evidence for business reconciliation and downstream restore planning.

**Supplier outcome:** an affected-population and exception report tied to the revised mapping version, with method, coverage, unresolved exceptions and source date.

**Boundary:** the report does not establish Saira's acceptance or Rosa's service readiness. Commercial completion remains governed by the contract and authorized supplier process.

**Decision rights:** Chen prepares technical evidence within the supplier's accepted assignment. Beck confirms supplier priority and resources. Saira decides whether business evidence is acceptable. Jules coordinates the interface and reports consequences.

**Inputs:** mapping version, source population, exception definition, data access and review format. Each must be confirmed.

**Acceptance:** Beck and Chen read back outcome, inputs, capacity, sample timing and full-package timing. Dates remain proposed until that confirmation exists.

## Support actions

| Need | Action | Owner / authority | Success evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence definition | State the reconciliation questions and usable report conditions | Saira | Written criteria and sample disposition |
| Data access | Confirm source population and access route | Technical or data owner to confirm | Reproducible population |
| Supplier priority | Confirm capacity and review time | Beck | Accepted supplier commitment |
| Interface control | Track version, questions and decisions in one record | Jules | Current evidence register |

## Review checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Sample definition | Version, population, method and exceptions visible | Correct misunderstanding before scale |
| Sample review | Saira records usable or unusable with reason | Proceed or revise |
| Full package | Coverage and exceptions reconcile to the agreed population | Route business acceptance |
| Repeated miss after accepted inputs and support | Factual chronology and migration impact | Use supplier governance for the contract or delivery decision |

**Repair:** putting Chen on an internal performance plan would cross the organizational boundary. The corrected delegation governs the supplier interface and routes formal supplier management through Beck and the contract.
