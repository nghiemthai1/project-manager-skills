# Relay: repair a technical workstream commitment

Fictional training scenario, 16 October 2026. Omar coordinates technical work for the B1 pilot. Two promised integration updates arrived after the dependency review, and the latest update revealed that the recovery environment was still unavailable. Mina is the project manager. Omar's line manager, formal performance process and accepted allocation are not supplied.

## Commitment diagnosis

| Condition | Evidence | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Update outcome | Technical control update expected before dependency review | Purpose is known; usable contents are only partly defined |
| Acceptance | Two dates were promised | Timing was accepted, but no evidence confirms the early-warning rule |
| Capacity and priority | Not supplied | Unknown; resource decision may be needed |
| Recovery environment | Unavailable when the latest update arrived | Confirmed input obstacle |
| Decision rights | Omar coordinates technical content; Lena owns security acceptance | Technical recommendation and acceptance must remain separate |
| Motive or reliability | No evidence | Do not infer character |

Recommended sequence:

1. Protect the project by marking environment and timing uncertain.
2. Renew the delegation around one usable control update and early-warning behavior.
3. Confirm Omar's allocation and the environment owner.
4. Review two cycles, then restore wider autonomy or escalate the remaining gap.

Mina may coordinate technical delivery and inspect project evidence. Nothing supplied authorizes a formal performance rating or employment consequence.

## Delegation brief

**Purpose:** make current technical evidence visible early enough for dependency, readiness and date decisions.

**Outcome:** a weekly technical control update identifying completed evidence, blocked inputs, forecast changes and decisions needed. A material blocker or forecast change is disclosed when known rather than held for the weekly update.

**Boundary:** Omar coordinates technical content within his accepted workstream role. Mina owns integrated reporting. Lena retains security acceptance. Ada retains applicable mandate decisions.

**Success evidence:** update arrives before the agreed review cutoff; every material blocker has a source, owner or ownership gap, impact, next action and disclosure date. The exact cutoff and allocation remain proposed until confirmed.

**Decision rights:** Omar may explain technical options and recommend sequence. He does not approve security evidence, scope or date changes. Mina may escalate integrated consequences but cannot invent capacity.

**Read-back:** Omar must confirm the outcome, cutoff, allocation and early-warning rule. Until then, the commitment is proposed.

## Support actions

| Need | Action | Owner / authority | Success evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recovery environment | Identify the provisioning owner and usable condition | Mina coordinates; performer and authority unknown | Named accepted owner and evidence of usability |
| Update burden | Consolidate duplicate reporting into one control format | Mina | One source used at the review |
| Capacity / priority | Confirm Omar's allocation and competing commitments | Resource or line manager with Omar | Explicit allocation or priority decision |
| Acceptance clarity | Confirm required recovery evidence | Lena | Applicable criterion and review lead time |

## Review checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Evidence | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Dry run | Complete update with explicit unknowns | Clarify format and missing support |
| Next dependency review | Update and material signals precede the accepted cutoff | Recognize follow-through or adjust the constraint |
| Following control cycle | Result repeats without close prompting | Restore wider autonomy |
| Repeated late material signal after accepted support | Chronology, impact and acceptance record | Protect delivery and seek the exact resource or line decision |

**Repair:** assigning Omar in RACI and adding another date would repeat the same mechanism. The corrected record makes acceptance, capacity, inputs and follow-through observable.
