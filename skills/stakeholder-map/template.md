## Authority and evidence checks

Keep formal authority separate from impact, interest and interpersonal support. Label inferred ratings and motives. Do not claim that workshop attendance grants decision power. Record missing groups and the action to identify a representative.

## Version and revisit record

Record changes in scope, organization, evidence and participation. Preserve prior placements with the reason for each change.
