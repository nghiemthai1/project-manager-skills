# Stakeholder Map: software example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Worked map

Ada authorizes project funding and material baseline changes. Priya owns product ordering and the benefit review. Lena accepts security evidence. Theo accepts service handover. Pilot-tenant administrators are highly affected by access recovery even if they have little internal organizational power.

Mina's engagement priorities are to confirm acceptance criteria with Lena and Theo, validate administrator recovery workflows, and give Ada clear funding/schedule decisions. Omar supplies technical feasibility evidence. These purposes differ; a single executive update will not meet all of them.

## Reasoning

A power-only map would place pilot administrators at the edge. Their experience is directly relevant to whether recovery access works, so impact justifies direct validation involvement.

## Repair

**Flawed:** "Ada is the most senior person, so her green light replaces security acceptance."

**Corrected:** "Ada sponsors the project; Lena's acceptance authority remains explicit. Escalate a conflict without silently transferring that authority."
