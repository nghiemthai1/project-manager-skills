# Stakeholder Map: migration example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Worked map

Noel owns funding and baseline decisions. Saira accepts business reconciliation. Rosa owns service acceptance. Chen leads migration engineering, and Beck supplies the vendor mapping. Service desk agents and archive users are affected groups whose operational needs must be validated.

Rosa's refusal to accept handover without restore evidence is recorded as a condition with a rationale, not as a negative personality assessment. Saira's reconciliation standard addresses record correctness, while service desk users can expose usability or missing-workflow problems.

## Reasoning

A vendor delivery receipt answers a different question from business acceptance or operational ownership. The stakeholder map preserves all three responsibilities.

## Repair

**Flawed:** "Vendor says done, so everyone else only needs to be informed."

**Corrected:** "Vendor delivery needs receiver validation, business acceptance, and service-owner acceptance before the relevant gates close."
