## Gap analysis

Sending agents a weekly bulletin may satisfy a narrow “keep informed” default but does not test whether migrated attachments, search, archive access and escalation routes support their work. The second grid changes the plan: propose representative workflow trials with explicit feedback disposition. Saira remains the business acceptance owner; participation does not silently transfer her authority.

Rosa's refusal to accept unproven restore is evidence about an unmet requirement, not evidence of a difficult personality. Before choosing persuasion tactics, obtain the restore criteria and execution evidence. Beck's confidence is supplier input, not independent proof of business or operational readiness.
