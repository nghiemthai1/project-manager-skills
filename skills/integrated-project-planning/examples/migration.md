# Integrated Project Planning: migration example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Northstar plan

The plan connects the agreed migration population to reconciliation and restore criteria. Vendor mapping feeds rehearsal. Rehearsal evidence feeds the cutover decision. Training and service ownership precede closure. Saira and Rosa have distinct acceptance roles.

B1 is approved under M-D001 on 5 October. After observed linkage failures and a deteriorating cost forecast, Jules develops a phased alternative rather than changing only the date. M-CR02 on 9 November authorizes phased scope, 28 November cutover, B2 USD 300,000, and funding.

The plan still requires passing restore evidence. The failed 25 November demonstration therefore holds the release even though the revised baseline was approved earlier.
