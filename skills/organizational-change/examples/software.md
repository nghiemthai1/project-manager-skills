## Phases 2–3: diagnose and demonstrate

For administrators, check awareness of the changed access path, knowledge of the steps and ability to perform them with real permissions. For operators, observe use of the recovery/reversal guidance in an appropriate rehearsal. If a person knows the steps but lacks role access, repair access; repeating the training is not the relevant response. Exact criteria, cohort and session timing remain to be agreed.

The security evidence is a separate required output owned through the delivery/acceptance process. Even successful operator practice cannot establish Lena's acceptance unless its evidence meets the actual security criterion and she records the decision.

Propose named support coverage, a feedback channel and escalation ownership; no invitations or live schedules are created. After actual handover, inspect recurring access/recovery problems and whether guidance works for the affected cohort. Do not infer adoption from the number of announcement recipients.
