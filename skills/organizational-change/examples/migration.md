# Northstar: training is not the same as usable capability

Fictional instructional extension as of 27 November 2026. The canonical successful rehearsal and Saira's acceptance are recorded that day; cutover/go and service handover are later events. The group counts below are additional teaching inputs, not recorded Northstar readiness data.

## Impact and diagnosis

Assume a proposed cohort of 12 service-desk agents across two shifts. All 12 attended a course; only 8 have completed an observed attachment-and-escalation exercise. Of those 8, 6 succeeded and 2 were blocked by missing role permissions. Four have not yet been observed.

| Evidence | Supported conclusion | Unsupported conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| 12/12 attendance | The supplied cohort attended | Everyone can perform the new task |
| 6/8 observed success | Six demonstrated the tested workflow | The remaining six are incapable or unwilling |
| 2 access blocks | Ability was constrained by permissions in those trials | More classroom training will solve the problem |
| 4 unobserved | Evidence missing | Assume success because peers passed |

## Workflow actions

1. **Map impacts:** Saira's process view and Rosa's support view should identify whether shifts or specialist roles need different workflows. Those participation requests remain proposed.
2. **Diagnose:** treat the two access failures as work-system barriers; obtain the actual access owner and a correction/retest plan. Ask the four unobserved users about practical availability rather than labelling them resistant.
3. **Demonstrate:** repeat the applicable tasks after access correction and observe the remaining critical cohort. Readiness criteria and authority determine whether a supported pilot is acceptable; this skill does not invent a pass threshold.
4. **Support:** after actual go authorization, provide agreed coverage for both shifts, record recurring questions and route incidents to their real owners. The 27 November business acceptance does not imply Rosa has already accepted service responsibility.
5. **Reinforce:** transfer the observed behavior measure and remaining actions to accepted business/service owners. Tie later outcome review to actual use and process performance rather than attendance.

**Repair:** “100% trained” is a valid activity statistic but an incomplete readiness claim. The corrected artifact shows the tested behavior, access failures, unobserved population and decisions required before withdrawing support.
