# Relay lesson: plan proof alongside implementation

Fictional training scenario. Practice state: proposed from observed evidence, not validated across releases. The lesson concerns acceptance-evidence planning, not a universal meeting rule.

Expected outcome: a ready pilot on the applicable 30 October baseline. Observed sequence: Lena rejects missing recovery demonstration on 28 October; D-004 defers to 3 November on 29 October; evidence is accepted on 2 November and pilot begins on 3 November. CR-001's earlier scope/funding approval did not itself establish readiness.

The observation supports a narrow finding: required implementation and planning approvals are insufficient when decision-critical evidence is missing. It does not establish who caused the gap or prove that increasing meeting frequency would prevent recurrence. Original evidence plans, prerequisites and earlier warnings are needed to diagnose the mechanism.

Proposed practice: plan each required acceptance demonstration, its prerequisites, actual acceptor and useful review lead time alongside delivery work. Use it where a release decision depends on evidence requiring execution, correction or independent review. A trivial repeatable change with already applicable automated evidence may need a different coordination pattern; no identical cadence is prescribed.
