## Authority and feasibility notes

Beck's proposed A/R covers the vendor mapping deliverable only. It must be confirmed against the supplier's actual authority and contract; a vendor contact does not automatically bind the supplier. Chen's technical verification is distinct from Saira accepting business results and Rosa accepting service responsibility. Restore execution is proposed for Chen pending skills, environment access and available capacity.

A cutover go/no-go row remains unresolved at this planning point. The scenario later records Noel's 28 November go decision, but the early draft must not backfill that later event as current authorization. Confirm the applicable authority before execution.
