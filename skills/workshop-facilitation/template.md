## Context and assumptions

Separate supplied facts, hypotheses, proposals and unknowns. Credit inline inputs as answers; keep question-progress labels honest.

## Pause/resume state

## Closeout

State the output, actual decisions, open questions and next action. Participation or silence is not approval.
