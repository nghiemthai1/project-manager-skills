## Method foundations

- Roger Fisher, William Ury and Bruce Patton, interest-based negotiation: separate people from the problem, focus on interests, create options and use objective criteria.
- Best alternative to a negotiated agreement, reservation boundaries and possible agreement zones for testing whether a package is preferable to no agreement.
- Voluntary, neutral mediation with explicit confidentiality and authority limits.
- Project governance: responsibility, recommendation, acceptance and decision rights remain distinct.

Framework names do not establish certification, field reliability or endorsement by the source authors.
