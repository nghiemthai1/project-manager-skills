# Relay: negotiate the recovery-evidence dispute

Fictional training scenario, 16 October 2026. The B1 pilot target is 30 October. Omar wants to preserve the date and believes the recovery work can be completed during final integration. Lena says security acceptance requires an applicable recovery demonstration before readiness. Mina is the project manager; Ada approved the B1 mandate but has not waived the security condition.

## Conflict assessment

The observed conflict is not “engineering versus a blocker.” The parties disagree about sequencing, evidence and the credibility of the target.

| Item | Omar perspective | Lena perspective | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recovery work | Can be completed during final integration | Must be demonstrated before acceptance | Positions are evidenced; detailed effort and criteria remain unknown |
| Date | Preserve 30 October | Date cannot replace evidence | Target exists; feasibility is not established |
| Authority | Omar owns technical preparation | Lena owns the relevant security acceptance | Ada's mandate approval is not a security result or waiver |

Primary mechanism: interest and sequence conflict with an information gap. Direct structured negotiation fits if both parties can participate. An authority escalation is reserved for an actual scope, date, funding or exception decision.

## Interests and boundaries

| Party | Position | Interests | Constraints / unknowns | No-agreement alternative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Omar | Keep the date and finish recovery during integration | Delivery credibility, coherent technical sequence, avoid rework | Environment availability, effort and exact evidence timing are unknown | Prepare the best feasible technical plan and expose a date exception if required |
| Lena | Do not accept until recovery is demonstrated | Secure recovery, accountable acceptance, usable evidence | Exact scenario and review lead time need confirmation | Maintain rejection and document missing evidence |
| Mina | Obtain a feasible plan and decision | Integrated delivery, transparent tradeoffs, intact working relationship | Coordinates but cannot assign acceptance or waive controls | Escalate a bounded decision with evidence |

Shared interest: release a pilot that can recover safely and does not create a false commitment.

Objective criteria: R-ACC03, the applicable security acceptance rule, executed test evidence, confirmed environment availability and an integrated remaining forecast. The security criterion is not negotiable by the project team unless an actual exception authority is evidenced.

## Options

| Package | Benefit | Cost / condition | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continue current sequence | Avoids immediate replanning | Does not establish applicable evidence or date feasibility | No new authority, but no credible agreement |
| Define the recovery scenario now, secure the environment and execute before readiness | Tests the real acceptance path early | May reveal date or capacity impact | Technical and reviewer commitments must be confirmed |
| Propose deferral of optional polish to release relevant capacity | Could protect the mandatory evidence path | Exact resource release is unknown and scope remains B1 until changed | Actual scope authority |
| Revise the pilot date after the remaining network is assessed | Allows full evidence if timing is the constraint | Affects external commitment | Actual date authority |

Recommend the second package first, with the third and fourth retained as conditional decisions once capacity and schedule evidence exist.

## Conversation plan

Opening: “We share the goal of a recoverable pilot. The current record has a 30 October target and no completed recovery demonstration. Let us agree the applicable scenario, evidence, sequence and review lead time, then show any remaining decision honestly.”

Questions:

1. Lena, which recovery scenario and evidence establish R-ACC03 for this pilot?
2. Omar, what environment, input and skilled effort does that scenario require?
3. What can be executed before readiness, and what schedule evidence supports that answer?
4. If the current boundary is infeasible, which option should Mina take to the actual authority?

Pause trigger: either party starts arguing about motives rather than the evidence. Escalation trigger: a credible plan crosses B1 scope, funding or date authority, or an exception to the security rule is requested.

## Outcome record

No agreement is invented in this preparation artifact. Proposed close:

- Lena confirms the applicable recovery scenario and review evidence.
- Omar prepares the technical execution and timing basis.
- Mina reconciles capacity and schedule impacts.
- The parties reconvene for a read-back before the readiness decision.
- Any scope, date, funding or control exception is recorded separately by its actual authority.

Success evidence is an agreed criterion, executed applicable demonstration and explicit acceptance disposition. A calmer meeting alone is not resolution.

**Repair:** “Security agreed not to block the date” would erase the acceptance boundary. The corrected record keeps the control, timing evidence and decision route distinct.
