# Northstar: mediate supplier and receiving-team disagreement

Fictional training scenario, 6 November 2026. A migration lost attachment links. Supplier lead Beck says a patch corrects the mapping and wants the delivery marked complete. Saira cannot accept the migrated business result without reconciliation, and Rosa will not accept service responsibility until restore and monitoring demonstrations pass. Jules coordinates the project; Noel holds material plan and funding authority.

## Conflict assessment

The dispute combines information, process and interest conflicts. “Complete” refers to three different boundaries: supplier deliverable, business acceptance and service readiness. A facilitated mediation fits because bilateral conversations have repeated the same positions and the receiving roles remain necessary.

| Observed fact | Interpretation to avoid | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| A patch was delivered | All affected records are corrected | Version, population and reconciliation result |
| Attachment links were lost | The patch is ineffective everywhere | Reproduction, correction and justified coverage |
| Restore and monitoring proof are absent | Operations is refusing ownership | Agreed scenarios and executed results |
| Target and funding remain governed | The PM can trade acceptance for schedule | Actual decision rights and authorized options |

## Interests and alternatives

| Party | Position | Interests | Executable alternative if no agreement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beck / supplier | Mark the patched delivery complete | Contract clarity, bounded responsibility, avoid unending rework | Submit evidence and seek contractual disposition through the agreed route |
| Saira / business | Do not accept until reconciliation is credible | Correct business records and traceable exceptions | Maintain business rejection with documented gaps |
| Rosa / operations | Do not accept service until restore and monitoring pass | Recoverable, supportable service | Maintain service-transfer hold |
| Jules / project | Reach a usable plan | Feasible delivery and preserved relationships | Escalate a precise plan/funding/date decision |

Shared interest: establish what the patch corrected, finish the required evidence and close each boundary without transferring unknown obligations.

## Mediation plan

Facilitator: a neutral person who does not own the supplier claim or either acceptance decision. Jules may facilitate only if all parties accept the role and Jules can remain process-neutral.

Sequence:

1. Read back the three completion boundaries and their authorities.
2. Let each party state the observed event, impact and required result.
3. Agree the affected population, patch version and evidence questions.
4. Generate packages for correction, reconciliation, restore/monitoring and timing.
5. Record supplier disposition, business acceptance and service acceptance separately.

Private caucus information is not shared without the agreed permission. If a contract interpretation remains disputed, route it to the authorized commercial or legal process rather than deciding it in mediation.

## Candidate package

| Term | Proposed owner or authority | Evidence | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identify affected population and patch version | Beck with receiving technical review | Reproducible mapping and version record | Proposed |
| Execute justified reconciliation | Technical performer to confirm; Saira accepts business result | Population, exceptions and results | Proposed |
| Execute restore and monitoring scenarios | Technical/support roles to confirm; Rosa accepts service readiness | Demonstration results and residuals | Proposed |
| Reforecast remaining work and cost | Jules coordinates | Integrated sequence, capacity and funding | Proposed |
| Decide material baseline change if needed | Noel within actual delegation | Options, impacts and recommendation | Conditional |

No party has accepted this package merely by attending. The mediator closes with a term-by-term read-back and confirmation state.

## Follow-up

Checkpoints are correction evidence, business reconciliation, operational demonstrations and the authorized plan decision. Reopen the agreement if affected records remain unknown, a required result fails, assigned capacity is not accepted or residual obligations lack a receiver.

**Repair:** “The vendor delivered the patch, so operations owns the service” collapses three independent boundaries. The corrected mediation gives each authority a separate evidence-based disposition.
