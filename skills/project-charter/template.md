## Authority and escalation

PM delegation, sponsor decisions, distinct acceptance authorities, material tolerance gaps and the route for unresolved scope/date/funding changes.

## Decision and handoff

Actual decision or requested approval; exact authorized scope; conditions; approver/date/reference; next planning artifact. A blank signature line is not approval.
