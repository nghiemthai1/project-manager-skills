# Northstar charter — approved mandate summary with planning gaps

Fictional training artifact. Version M-CH-1.0, 5 October 2026. Jules summarizes the supplied approval M-D001; this document does not create a second approval or grant new authority. Noel sponsors, Chen leads migration, Beck leads vendor delivery, Saira accepts business results and Rosa accepts service handover.

## Outcome and scope

Move the agreed active service-desk work to the hosted platform with usable records, attachments, controlled access, recoverability, trained support and continuing archive access. Historical analytics redesign is excluded. The scenario does not establish a cost-saving target or a promise that every historical record moves into the active platform.

## Authorized comparison points

M-D001 approves cutover 21 November 2026 and performance budget USD 240,000. USD 24,000 management reserve is separate and sponsor-controlled. No release of that reserve is implied. Forecasts may change as evidence improves; only an authorized change revises the baseline.

## Success and continuing ownership

Business acceptance requires reconciliation of the agreed population, no unexplained high-severity mismatches and meaningful workflow evidence. Restore must be demonstrated and service acceptance obtained. The scenario establishes these requirements but not every sampling rule, severity rubric or recovery threshold; those remain to be agreed before claiming a pass.

Project performance compares original and revised scope/date/cost with actuals. Later operational benefits need a baseline, measure, accountable business owner and review schedule. Saira is a proposed benefit owner at this planning point; the later assigned benefit-review date should not be backfilled as if already decided on 5 October.

## Governance, assumptions and planning handoff

Noel owns material baseline/funding decisions. Jules coordinates delivery and escalation, with numerical delegated tolerances not supplied. Saira's business acceptance and Rosa's operational acceptance are separate; Beck's delivery statement cannot replace either. Confirm the final go/no-go and exception process before cutover.

Key uncertainties: usable vendor mapping, data-link integrity, recovery behavior, available migration capacity and affected support workflows. Jules should bring these to risk and integrated planning, with accepted owners and review dates as they become known. Neither a named lead nor a calendar target establishes feasible allocation.

The next artifact is the integrated plan: WBS including assurance and transition, traceable acceptance, a resource-feasible schedule, cost forecast and control cadence. Existing M-D001 authority is preserved; unresolved details are visible rather than used as a reason to invent a new charter approval.
