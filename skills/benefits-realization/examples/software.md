## Snapshot 2 — illustrative review on 4 December

Assume an evidenced teaching dataset: baseline 40 comparable tickets took 600 total minutes (15 minutes/ticket); post-pilot 50 took 600 minutes (12 minutes/ticket). Mean handling time fell 3 minutes, or 20% relative to 15. At the observed post-pilot volume, baseline-equivalent effort would be 50 × 15 = 750 minutes; observed effort is 600, a difference of 150 minutes or 2.5 hours.

This is an estimated capacity difference for this cohort/window, not cash saved or a forecast annual saving. The data do not establish causation: ticket mix, operator experience and concurrent support changes could matter. Reopened-ticket evidence and ongoing operating cost are not supplied, so net benefit remains incomplete. More tickets with identical total effort is consistent with the hypothesis, but is not proof that every access journey improved.
