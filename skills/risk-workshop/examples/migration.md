# Risk Workshop: migration example

Fictional training scenario. Values and thresholds are examples, not defaults for real projects.

## Guided workshop before rehearsal

**Facilitator:** "Assume cutover is technically complete but support cannot use its records. What might explain that?"

**Chen:** "Attachments could be associated with the wrong tickets."

## Worked prioritization

M-R01 addresses attachment identifier integrity. A separate recoverability risk requires a restore demonstration. These need different evidence and responses, so do not merge them into "data migration risk."

The workshop proposes representative linkage tests, explicit exception rules, and restore validation. It records residual uncertainty and acceptance authority. On 6 November, reproduced missing links become an issue. On 25 November, the failed restore demonstration blocks the release gate under the agreed criteria.
