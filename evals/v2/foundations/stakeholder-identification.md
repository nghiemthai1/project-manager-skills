## Bias and coverage review

The first list privileges management and vendor coordination. It omits the supplied affected shifts, outsourced workers and accessibility-dependent users; a manager cannot automatically represent all their needs. Why they were omitted is unknown. No demographic, disability diagnosis, motive or formal power is inferred.

Primary effects: changed operator/support/user workflows. Secondary effects to investigate: people depending on service responses and systems receiving migrated data. Tertiary effects to investigate: sustained service trust or organizational obligations; no particular harm is asserted. Other locations/shifts, nonstandard access routes and historical-data users remain discovery questions, not invented confirmed groups.
