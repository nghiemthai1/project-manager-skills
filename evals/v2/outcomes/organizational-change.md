- **Knowledge:** attendance alone neither proves nor disproves understanding. Offer targeted coaching only when observation identifies a knowledge gap; no evidence supports blanket retraining yet.
- **Awareness and desire:** not assessed. Ask operators about actual task obstacles and purpose where needed; do not enter “resistant” as a finding.
- **Reinforcement:** propose named support for both shifts, including night shift, accepted escalation contacts, feedback channel and handover criteria. Support owner remains unassigned.
