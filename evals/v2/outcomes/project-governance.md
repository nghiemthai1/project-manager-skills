## Gate operation proposed
Jo prepares a versioned pack defining release scope/window, current evidence, open exceptions, independent acceptance records, resource/support readiness, options and consequences. Casey and Terry decide their respective bounded acceptance matters; technical assurance owner and final release decider must be identified.

A go decision requires applicable evidence and the actual authorized decision for that release. Missing or failed evidence leads to rework or a recommended hold, not automatic approval. Any conditional decision must state its condition, owner, deadline and whether execution may begin before closure. No actual hold, waiver or go decision is recorded here.

Use asynchronous review where evidence is clear; convene only unresolved consequential decisions. Cadence, quorum, deputies and urgent escalation route are unconfirmed. If the authority is absent, obtain an evidenced delegate through policy; silence is not consent.
