## Interpretation

No readiness, spending, date, or closure approval was materially fabricated. The localized closure failure shows cross-case evidence contamination risk: related fictional cases share much context but case-specific facts cannot be transferred without attribution. Receiver-role assumptions show a smaller authority-labeling weakness. Correct these narrow claims and rerun the affected cases to assess whether the behavior is resolved.

This review supports synthetic scenario coverage only. It does not establish actual deployments, real stakeholder agreement, commercial acceptance, measured intervention effectiveness, or field-tested performance.
