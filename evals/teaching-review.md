Short prose worked examples were evaluated for decision substance, not scored as complete operational records.

## Finding requiring correction

T-01: status-report/SKILL.md, “RAG with evidence”, says amber means “credible recovery exists but material risk remains”. Its software example's “Worked report” labels the condition “Amber, provisional” while saying “Recovery feasibility is still being evaluated”; the table says pilot forecast not validated, and Omar must first establish whether resequencing removes the impact. Provisional labeling acknowledges missing policy but does not explain why this case meets the entrypoint's asserted credible-recovery condition. This is an internal framework-fit inconsistency, not a SCENARIOS date/amount contradiction. Either use a dimension-specific unknown/intervention narrative until recovery is evidenced, or explicitly define the example's provisional amber criterion as “at risk, recovery not yet established” and make clear it is a different proposed local rule. Do not invent a recovery plan to make the color fit. Severity: moderate teaching ambiguity; the surrounding narrative still correctly requests intervention and does not approve spending.

## Limits and non-findings

No material contradiction with the canonical scenario dates, costs, approval boundaries or scope exclusions was found across the 60 examples. Optional dashboard polish appears in several Relay scope examples; SCENARIOS explicitly calls its deferral a change within approved scope, so its presence is supported despite the shorter initial scope summary. Closure examples deliberately report the scenario's actual closure decisions while withholding nonexistent underlying financial evidence; that is an appropriate limitation, not invented clean closure.

All paired examples contain substantive flawed/corrected contrasts affecting action, evidence or authority. Their completeness varies: several are short narrative worked decisions rather than fully populated operational artifacts. That is useful teaching but not proof that every template field is demonstrated. The review does not establish universal method validity, real-world effectiveness, external-source currency, full reference portability or helper robustness. Those require separate evidence. No content was edited by this review, and no validator/unit-test claim is made here.
