## Source and response rules
Every communication carries as-of date, baseline version, actual evidence, forecast/proposal labels, and a link/reference to the same controlled record. Sol should nominate the repository/location; none was supplied. Keep detailed test results and decision references in that record, and summarize only relevant implications.

Set a response deadline for each decision request based on its actual lead time. If unknown, label the deadline proposed and agree it; do not invent a contractual cutoff. After a missed response, Sol checks receipt and obstacles, follows up directly, then escalates an unresolved consequence to the confirmed authority. Neither silence nor an acknowledged deck closes an acceptance item.

## Access and effectiveness
Use role-appropriate access for ticket content, attachments, personnel allocations, and vendor/commercial details; sensitivity policy is not supplied. Share only necessary examples and confirm accessible formats, time zones, and user availability. Do not scatter confidential source detail across broad decks.

Review after two proposed weekly cycles: decisions resolved by needed-by dates, handoffs explicitly acknowledged, repeated misunderstandings, and unresolved acceptance questions. Combine or remove redundant meetings by agreement. Record whether the plan improves decisions, not merely whether updates were sent.
