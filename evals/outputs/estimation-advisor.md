# Estimation recommendation

**Use a three-point PERT effort estimate for provisional planning. No five-day guarantee or defensible 95% confidence date is available.** The decision requested is a delivery commitment; the supplied inputs support an effort estimate only.

Scope is stated to be the same task across the three estimates. The task description, completion criteria, exclusions, and scenario assumptions were not supplied. Validate these with the estimators before adopting the estimate. Availability and the third-party sandbox remain unresolved.

| Input | Value | Basis / validation |

| Most likely M | 5 person-days | Supplied; not a guaranteed duration |
| Pessimistic P | 14 person-days | Supplied; confirm adverse-case boundary and whether sandbox waiting was excluded |

| Sandbox readiness | Pending, date unknown | Third-party contact and internal owner unnamed |

PERT fits three supplied values for a bounded, consistently scoped task and weights the typical case: **(2 + 4×5 + 14) / 6 = 6 person-days**. The supplied scenario range is **2–14 person-days**, not a validated confidence interval or guaranteed minimum/maximum. A triangular mean would be 7 person-days; method choice changes the central value, reinforcing that it is a planning convention.

The PERT spread heuristic is (14−2)/6 = 2 person-days. It does not establish a normal distribution or justify a “mean plus two spreads” 95% date. There is no calibrated probability model, delivery start, working calendar, verified capacity, or sandbox readiness date. Person-days cannot be relabeled as elapsed days.

An empirical throughput forecast would become useful with comparable completed work and stable flow data; none was supplied. Bottom-up sizing would help after acceptance criteria and integration work are decomposed. If sandbox uncertainty dominates the scope itself, switch first to an investigation with the exit question: “Can the task be completed in the available sandbox under the stated acceptance criteria?” The investigation duration also needs agreement, not invention.

**Commitment status:** retain five elapsed days as the director's desired target, not an approved or supported commitment. Propose a provisional planning allowance of six person-days with the stated range and dependencies. Before promising a delivery date, confirm scope/completion, capacity, sandbox availability, sequence, and calendar; then agree a risk tolerance and date with the accountable owner. No owner or deadline for that validation has yet been supplied. Re-estimate when sandbox capability, availability, scope, or actual progress changes. A 95% date would additionally need a justified probabilistic model and dependency assumptions.
