# Two recurring training projects

All people, organizations, amounts, and records in these scenarios are fictional. The dates and thresholds are training choices. They are not policy for a user's project.

## Software release: Relay service access

Relay is adding enterprise single sign-on to an existing service. Mina is project manager, Priya is product owner, Omar is engineering lead, Lena is security lead, Theo is service owner, and Ada is sponsor.

Approved scope v1 includes SAML login for one pilot tenant, audit events, recovery access, operator guidance, and a reversible rollout. SCIM provisioning is excluded. The target pilot date is 30 October 2026. The target is a planning objective until Ada approves the integrated baseline.

Baseline B1 is approved on 2 October: 30 October pilot, USD 100,000 performance budget, with scope above. A separate USD 10,000 sponsor-controlled management reserve is outside that baseline. The approval is decision D-001. Examples before 2 October must not claim B1 exists.

At the 16 October control point, platform delivery of the audit-event interface is forecast for 22 October, while the integration team needs it on 20 October. This is a two-calendar-day delivery gap, not CPM float. R-001 is the risk of interface incompatibility; I-001 is a current unavailable test environment. They are distinct problems. Mina owns coordination; Omar owns technical remediation.

Reporting-period values are PV 50,000, EV 40,000, AC 48,000, BAC 100,000, all USD and cumulative through 16 October. CV is -8,000, SV is -10,000, CPI is 0.833333..., and SPI is 0.8. A CPI-based EAC is 120,000. These values do not predict a calendar finish date.

CR-001 proposes deferring optional operator dashboard polish within the approved scope to protect the pilot. On 19 October Ada approves that scope change and a USD 120,000 revised performance budget B2 after resolving funding, including USD 10,000 additional authorization beyond the original total envelope. The 16 October report remains against B1. The scope change does not waive security acceptance. On 28 October security rejects readiness because recovery access has not been demonstrated. D-004 on 29 October defers the pilot to 3 November. Security evidence is accepted on 2 November. The pilot begins on 3 November, service handover completes on 6 November, and project closure is approved on 10 November. Benefit review is assigned to Priya for 4 December.

## IT migration: Northstar service desk

Northstar is migrating a service desk to a new hosted platform. Jules is project manager, Chen is migration lead, Saira is business process owner and acceptance authority, Beck is vendor lead, Rosa is operations owner, and Noel is sponsor.

Scope v1 includes active tickets, agreed attachments, access mapping, reconciliation, support training, and an archive-access route. Historical analytics redesign is excluded. A hybrid approach combines iterative migration rehearsals with controlled cutover approval.

B1 is approved on 5 October 2026: cutover 21 November, USD 240,000 performance budget, USD 24,000 separate management reserve, decision M-D001. Acceptance requires reconciliation of the agreed population, no unexplained high-severity mismatches, demonstrated restore, and support-owner acceptance. Counts alone do not prove record correctness.

At 23 October, PV is 80,000, EV 60,000, AC 75,000, BAC 240,000, all cumulative USD. CV is -15,000, SV is -20,000, CPI is 0.8, SPI is 0.75, and CPI-based EAC is 300,000. The USD 36,000 gap beyond the USD 264,000 total authorized envelope is an escalation, not assumed approval to spend.

M-DEP01: vendor export mapping expected 4 November, needed 2 November. M-R01: inconsistent attachment identifiers might break record linkage. On 6 November a rehearsal confirms actual lost attachment links, logged separately as M-I002 and linked to the risk. Rosa will not accept service responsibility until the restore and monitoring demonstrations pass.

Noel approves M-CR02 on 9 November: phased migration, cutover 28 November, B2 performance budget USD 300,000 with funding explicitly authorized. Original baselines remain in history. Restore demonstration fails on 25 November, so the release gate holds. A successful rehearsal and Saira's acceptance are recorded on 27 November. Cutover proceeds on 28 November under Noel's go decision. Operations accepts handover on 2 December; project closes on 4 December; Saira owns the 15 January 2027 benefit review.

## Using the examples

Examples identify their point in this timeline. Earlier artifacts retain the information available at that time. Later examples must not backfill approval into an earlier report. Local variations, such as a toy CPM network, are explicitly labeled instructional subcases and do not replace these project dates.
