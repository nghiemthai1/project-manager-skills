## Definition of depth

- Components: field definitions, decision rules, completed deliverables, quality checks and repaired failure cases.
- Interactive skills: entry modes, three to five adaptive questions, explicit branches, numbered recommendations and a final artifact. Supplied context skips already answered questions.
- Workflows: phase inputs/outputs, entry/exit evidence, linked skill handoffs, branching decisions and an end-to-end worked run.
- All packages: framework fit, alternatives and limits; meaningful failure consequences and corrections; two domain-specific examples. Software and IT migration remain the library's domains.
- Operational artifacts must be usable: full RACI grids, rendered Gantt examples with source data, complete registers and decision records. Word count and headings alone do not establish quality.
