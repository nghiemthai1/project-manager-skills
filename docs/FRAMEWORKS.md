# Frameworks and their limits

A framework earns its place by improving a decision. Use the method that fits the evidence, consequence, and uncertainty. A recognizable name does not make the input reliable.

| Method | Useful decision | Required basis | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deliverable-oriented WBS | What work belongs in scope? | Mandate, boundaries, completion evidence | Completeness of decomposition does not prove all uncertainty is resolved |
| Three-point estimation | How should a bounded uncertain task be sized? | Consistent scope/unit and explained scenarios | PERT's spread convention is not a validated confidence interval |
| Critical Path Method | Which modeled activities govern finish? | Durations and acyclic dependency logic | Simple CPM does not resolve calendars or scarce-resource conflicts |
| Power/interest plus impact | Who needs which engagement? | Observed roles, authority, concerns, and affectedness | Low formal power does not imply low importance |
| Interest-based negotiation and mediation | Can competing project interests reach a bounded agreement? | Stated positions, evidenced interests, executable alternatives, criteria and authority | A facilitator cannot manufacture consent, waive controls or grant decision authority |
| Delegation, feedback and coaching loops | What leadership response will improve a recurring project result? | Accepted outcome, observed behavior, capacity, support, reporting relationship and authority | Project leadership does not confer formal line-management or employment authority |
| RACI | Who performs and owns a defined deliverable? | Actual organizational authority | A matrix cannot grant authority or prove acceptance |
| DACI | Who drives, decides, contributes to, and receives a decision? | A clear question and decision authority | Approval and implementation are separate events |
| Probability/impact review | Which risks deserve attention first? | Defined scale and evidence confidence | Ordinal score products are not monetary exposure |
| Premortem | What plausible failures are being overlooked? | A bounded future outcome and varied perspectives | Imagined stories are hypotheses, not observed events |
| Capacity analysis | Is demand feasible for the available people? | Common period, complete allocations, skills and timing | Aggregate spare hours can hide a specialist overload |
| Earned value | Is cost/progress performance diverging from the baseline? | Objective earning rules and aligned cumulative data | Value-based schedule variance is not calendar delay |
| Scrum forecasting | What coherent increment is feasible this Sprint? | Product context, Done, team history, and availability | Velocity is not a cross-team or individual productivity metric |
| Traceability | Does the evidence support each requirement and change? | Stable IDs, criteria, results, authority | A linked planned test is not a passed test |
| Change control | Should a baseline boundary change? | Impact, options, delegated authority | Forecast updates alone are not approvals |
| Readiness gates | Does this release have the required evidence? | Applicable criteria, results, recovery, ownership | A majority of passes cannot erase a mandatory failure |
| Retrospective / PDCA | Which improvement should be tried and inspected? | Observations, hypothesis, owner, measure | One successful cycle does not establish universal effectiveness |
| Evidence-linked memory | What is known now and how did it change? | Ordered sources, claim evidence, preserved history | Plausible attribution is not verified identity |

## References for verification

These links explain technical methods and specifications. Original methods and examples coexist with explicitly licensed close adaptations. See the [source notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and each adapted package’s SOURCE.md.

- [GAO Schedule Assessment Guide](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-16-89g): integrated schedule quality, dependency logic, and critical-path interpretation.
- [DOE integrated project management using earned value](https://www.energy.gov/documents/integrated-project-management-using-earned-value-management-system): earned-value measures and forecast assumptions.
- [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html): Scrum's accountabilities, events, artifacts, and commitments.
- [DACI decision framework](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/daci): decision roles and a structured decision process.
- [Orange Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/orange-book): risk management principles and governance.
- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification): package structure and metadata.

No skill claims certification or compliance with a standard merely by referring to one. Validate the organization's own policies, authority, and acceptance conditions when using a method.
